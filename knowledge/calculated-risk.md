# Session: The Calculated Risk

The Calculated Risk is the flagship corporate session.

Core business question: When should a team act aggressively, when should it wait, and how can leaders distinguish a calculated risk from a reckless move?

The chess analogy is a sound sacrifice. Strong players sometimes give up material because they receive something more valuable in return: initiative, time, activity, information, attacking chances, or a more favorable future position. The lesson is not "take more risk." The lesson is to understand what is being exchanged.

Decision lenses: upside, downside, reversibility, confidence, initiative, and time horizon.

Useful language:
- Calculated Attack: upside is meaningful, downside is manageable, and reversibility or learning value is high enough to justify action.
- Improve the Position: the opportunity is real, but the team should first reduce uncertainty or create a smaller test.
- Don't Force It: the downside dominates and boldness alone does not improve the position.

The website's Risk Lab is an illustrative teaching tool. It is not a validated scientific model and must not be presented as financial or investment advice.
