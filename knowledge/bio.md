# Prasanna Rao — speaker background

Prasanna Rao is an International Master in chess. He has competed internationally for more than 15 years, represented India, and earned three Grandmaster norms.

His professional career spans analytics, strategy, operations, product management, and applied AI. His experience includes roles at Vida Health, Split, and Amazon.

The speaking proposition combines elite competitive chess, real business and product leadership, and practical decision-making under uncertainty.

The purpose of the sessions is not to teach chess notation or opening theory. Chess is used as a memorable decision environment for discussing risk, tradeoffs, initiative, optionality, time pressure, second-order effects, and judgment.
