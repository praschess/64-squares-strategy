# Session: Think in Positions, Not Moves

Core business question: How do teams avoid optimizing one visible decision while making the overall system worse?

In chess, a move cannot be judged in isolation. A superficially attractive move can weaken the king, lose control of an important square, reduce mobility, or create a problem several moves later.

The business translation is to evaluate the position before optimizing the move.

Position checklist:
- What are our durable advantages?
- What are our constraints?
- What is the opponent, customer, market, or partner likely to do next?
- Which options remain available after this decision?
- What second-order effects are we creating?
- What information would change the decision?
- Are we solving the local problem while worsening the broader system?

This session is useful for product prioritization, strategy, resource allocation, organizational decisions, and teams facing several competing options.
