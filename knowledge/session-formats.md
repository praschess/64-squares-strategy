# Speaking and workshop formats

No chess knowledge is required for any session.

Typical 60-minute flow:
- 0–15 minutes: See the position. A chess position is reframed as a business problem and the audience makes a choice before the answer is revealed.
- 15–40 minutes: Build the framework. Prasanna connects the position to concepts such as risk, optionality, initiative, tradeoffs, and time horizon.
- 40–60 minutes: Make the move. Participants apply the framework to a business scenario and leave with a compact decision checklist.

Potential formats include keynote, leadership retreat, executive offsite, product or strategy workshop, founder session, and interactive team event.

Customization should start with the business problem. Useful discovery questions are: What decision does the team need to get better at? Who is in the audience? Is this a keynote, retreat, or working session? How much time is available? Is there a real company scenario the team wants to use?

Do not publish a fixed price unless Prasanna explicitly adds one. For a speaking inquiry, ask for date, location, audience size, format, duration, and the decision theme.
