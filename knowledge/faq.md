# FAQ

## Do participants need to know chess?
No. The sessions are designed for business audiences and use chess positions visually and intuitively. The goal is decision-making, not chess instruction.

## Is this a motivational speech?
The intent is more practical than motivational. The sessions use interactive decisions and reusable frameworks.

## Can the session be customized?
Yes. The strongest customization starts from a real business challenge such as risk-taking, prioritization, leadership under uncertainty, or decision-making under pressure.

## Is the Risk Lab a scientific risk model?
No. It is an illustrative teaching exercise that helps people discuss confidence, upside, downside, and reversibility.

## What session should a leadership team choose?
If the central issue is risk-taking or bold decisions, start with The Calculated Risk. If the issue is competing priorities or system-level strategy, use Think in Positions, Not Moves. If the issue is speed, uncertainty, or high-pressure decisions, use Decision-Making Under Pressure.

## What information is needed for a speaking inquiry?
Date, location, audience size, audience seniority, format, duration, and the business decision or challenge the organizer wants the session to address.
